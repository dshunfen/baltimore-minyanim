# Terms & Conditions

These apply to the Baltimore minyanim text-message service at (877) 471-3475.

- **The service:** text a time (such as `now` or `6am`) or `zmanim` and receive a
  reply with minyan times or zmanim for Baltimore. It is free and run by an
  individual as a community service.
- **Message frequency:** one reply for each text you send. You are never
  subscribed to anything and no recurring messages are sent.
- **Cost:** the service does not charge. Message and data rates from your mobile
  carrier may apply.
- **Help:** text `help` for a list of commands.
- **Stop:** text `STOP` to block all further messages from this number.
- **Accuracy:** times come from Baltimore Jewish Life listings and Hebcal and are
  provided as is, without any guarantee. Confirm with the shul when it matters.
- **Carriers:** mobile carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
- **Privacy:** see [PRIVACY.md](PRIVACY.md).
